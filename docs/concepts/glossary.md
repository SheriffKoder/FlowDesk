# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FlowDesk | SaaS product: 360° customer view for customer success teams |
| CSM | Customer Success Manager — primary user of Customer Health |
| Customer Health | Overview page: sortable/filterable customer table + detail drawer |
| Health score | Numeric signal of account health (table column) |
| Segment | Health bucket: **Healthy** / **Watch** / **At Risk** |
| MRR | Monthly recurring revenue (table column) |
| Owner | CSM responsible for the customer |
| Health detail | Per-customer payload from `GET /api/customers/{id}/health` (events, usage, notes) |
